# HR Attrition Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition and workforce data using Power BI to uncover patterns, understand factors associated with employee turnover, and provide insights that can support better HR decision-making.

The interactive dashboard explores workforce demographics, employee satisfaction, job characteristics, overtime, and attrition trends to better understand employee retention and potential turnover drivers.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall workforce composition and employee distribution.
- Measure employee attrition and retention rates.
- Identify departments and job roles with higher attrition rates.
- Explore factors associated with employee attrition, including job satisfaction, job level, work-life balance, and overtime.
- Examine overtime patterns and their association with employee turnover.
- Investigate outliers in employee-related data.

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
