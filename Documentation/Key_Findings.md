# HR Attrition Analysis | Power BI

## Dashboard Pages & Key Insights

### 1. Welcome Page

An interactive landing page introducing the dashboard and providing navigation to its main analytical sections.

**Key Highlights:**
- Retention Rate: 84%
- Attrition Rate: 16.1%
- Average Tenure: 7 Years
- Average Age: 37 Years
- Employees Stayed: 1,233
- Employees Left: 237

![Welcome Page](./Screenshots/Welcom.png)

### 2. Overview

Provides a high-level view of employee attrition, retention, department performance, and overtime patterns.

**Key Insights:**
- Total employees who left: 237.
- Sales recorded the highest department attrition rate at 20.6%.
- Human Resources followed with 19.0%.
- The 31–38 age group recorded the highest number of employee exits (74).
- Laboratory Technicians had the highest number of employee exits among job roles (62).
- Overtime distribution is examined across departments and attrition status.

![Overview](./Screenshots/Overview.png)

### 3. Employee Analysis

Explores the demographic and professional characteristics of the workforce.

**Key Insights:**
- Total Employees: 1,470.
- Employees Stayed: 1,233.
- Average Tenure: 7 Years.
- Average Monthly Income: 6,503.
- Research & Development represents the largest department with 961 employees.
- Male employees represent 60% of the workforce, while female employees represent 40%.
- Workforce distribution is analyzed by age group, education field, department, gender, and job role.

![Employee Analysis](./Screenshots/Employee%20Analysis.png)

### 4. Attrition Drivers

Explores the relationship between employee attrition and several workplace factors to identify patterns and potential areas of concern.

**Key Insights:**
- Employees with Job Satisfaction Level 1 recorded the highest attrition rate (22.8%).
- Employees at Job Level 1 had the highest attrition rate (26.3%).
- Employees with Work-Life Balance Level 1 recorded the highest attrition rate (31.3%).
- The 18–24 age group recorded the highest attrition rate (39.2%).
- Overtime patterns are analyzed in relation to employee attrition.

![Attrition Drivers](./Screenshots/Attrition%20Driver.png)

### 5. Outliers Analysis

Provides a detailed view of employee records identified as outliers using the Interquartile Range (IQR) method.

The 1.5 × IQR rule was applied to determine the lower and upper bounds for identifying unusually low or high Monthly Income values.

This analysis helps investigate unusual employee income values and examine records that may require further attention.

![Outliers Analysis](./Screenshots/outliers.png)
